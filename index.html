How the notes parser works

Lines starting Q: followed by A: become question/answer cards. Term - definition, Term: definition and Front | Back lines become term cards. Any leftover paragraph is split into sentences and turned into fill-in-the-blank (cloze) cards, blanking the longest word. Up to 50 cards per paste.

Free forever, no account, no server — the whole tool is one static page. Sample deck: 10 real cards from Pride and Prejudice by Jane Austen (1813, public domain). Not affiliated with Anki, Google, or Quizlet.
